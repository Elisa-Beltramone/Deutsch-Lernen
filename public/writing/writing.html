German Writing Practice

Choose your level and practice writing in German. Each level has a recommended word length to help you improve step by step.

A1 – Beginner

At the A1 level you write very simple sentences about yourself and everyday topics. You practice basic grammar and common vocabulary.

Typical length: 30–50 words

A2 – Elementary

At A2 you can write short paragraphs describing experiences, daily routines, or simple opinions. Sentences become longer and more connected.

Typical length: 60–80 words

B1 – Intermediate

At B1 you write structured paragraphs and short texts. You can explain opinions, describe events, and write informal emails.

Typical length: 100–140 words

B2 – Upper Intermediate

At B2 you can write detailed texts and arguments. You practice essays, formal emails, and structured explanations.

Typical length: 150–180 words
